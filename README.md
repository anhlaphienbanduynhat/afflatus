## 🚀 What Is afflatus?

afflatus is an end-user application for people who want to make AI art without losing control of their files. It runs on your own computer and works alongside your own ComfyUI setup. ComfyUI is the generation engine; afflatus is the studio in front of it.

The app is built around three main ideas:

- Pictures: create AI images from written ideas.
- Videos: turn still images or scenes into short video clips.
- Timelines: arrange pictures, clips, and scenes in a visual sequence.

Because afflatus is self-hosted, your projects stay on your machine. You are not forced into a cloud account. You can keep your generated media, project files, and working folders organized in one place.

afflatus does not replace ComfyUI — it works in front of it. That means your existing setup, models and workflows remain the engine behind everything you make in the studio.

In practice, this means a few simple habits:

- Start ComfyUI before you start working in afflatus.
- Keep ComfyUI running while you generate pictures or videos.
- If you change something inside ComfyUI, refresh afflatus so it picks up the change.
- Keep your model files in the locations ComfyUI already expects.

This arrangement is what makes afflatus flexible. You keep full ownership of your setup, and afflatus simply gives you a friendlier way to use it.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**afflatus does not open.** Download it again from the link above and open the new download. A partial or interrupted download is the most common cause.

**afflatus opens but cannot find ComfyUI.** Start ComfyUI first and confirm it is running on your computer. Then reopen afflatus or refresh the studio page.

**Image generation does not start.** Check that ComfyUI works on its own before trying again. afflatus depends on ComfyUI for generation, so ComfyUI must be ready first.

**Your computer feels slow while generating.** Close other heavy applications and try a smaller project. Image and video generation are demanding tasks, and giving them room to breathe makes a real difference.

**Your drive is filling up.** Generated media takes space. Move finished projects to a larger drive, or delete experiments you no longer need.

**A video clip looks wrong or stutters.** Shorten the clip, reduce the number of items on the timeline, and preview again.

## 💬 A Few Practical Tips

- Keep one project per idea rather than one giant project for everything.
- Save often, especially before long video generation runs.
- Back up your project folder to an external drive on a regular schedule.
- Experiment on small projects first, then scale up once you know what your computer handles comfortably.
